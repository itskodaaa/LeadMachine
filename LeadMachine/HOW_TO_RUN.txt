⚡ LEAD MACHINE ENTERPRISE EDITION (v2.0.0)
Autonomous B2B Lead Discovery, Reachability & Direct Outreach Engine
======================================================================

ONE-LINE INSTALLATION (ENTERPRISE WORKSTATIONS):
--------------------------------------------------
On any Windows machine, open PowerShell and execute:
  irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/itskodaaa/LeadMachine/master/install.ps1 | iex

Or from this directory:
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install.ps1

On macOS / Linux:
  curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/itskodaaa/LeadMachine/master/install.sh | bash

MANUAL LAUNCH:
--------------------------------------------------
Windows: Double-click "Launch_LeadMachine.bat"
macOS:   Double-click "Launch_LeadMachine.command"
Linux:   Run "./start_lead_machine.sh"

Dashboard opens automatically in your browser at:
  http://localhost:3333

KEY ENTERPRISE CAPABILITIES:
--------------------------------------------------
1. Autonomous Lead Hunter (Zero Manual Scraping):
   - Navigate to the "Lead Hunter" workspace.
   - Select your Target State (e.g., California, Illinois, Texas) and Target Industry / Category (e.g. HVAC, Roofing, Dental, Logistics).
   - Click "Launch Autonomous Discovery". The system automatically searches Google Maps, parses real-time business profiles, performs pre-flight reachability checks, and inserts verified leads directly into your database.

2. Full Corporate Identity Suite:
   - Configure your complete corporate profile: First Name, Last Name, Job Title, Company Name, Official Website, Full Street Address, Suite/Unit, City, State, ZIP, and Country.
   - Dynamic templates support rich message personalization variables:
     {company_name}, {first_name}, {city}, {state}, {full_name}, {job_title}, {company_sender}
   - Real-time Live Form Preview verifies what business contact forms will see before you send.

3. Enterprise Telemetry & Sandbox Mode:
   - Real-time SSE streaming of live outreach events, DOM interactions, and response metrics.
   - Sandbox Mode allows running test outreach campaigns without submitting forms.

4. CRM & Data Export:
   - Full lead search, filtering by status, bulk operations, and 1-click CSV export for Salesforce, HubSpot, or Excel.

5. In-Place Updates:
   - Built-in update engine allows instant software version verification and in-place updates.
